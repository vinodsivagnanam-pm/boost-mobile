FAQs

Do I need a data plan for my tablet?

While a data plan isn’t required to use your tablet, adding one will allow you to stay online wherever you are. Without a data plan, your tablet can only connect to the internet when Wi-Fi is available, which limits how and where you can use it.

With a Boost Mobile tablet data plan, you can browse the web, stream videos, use GPS, play online games, keep the kids entertained and stay connected anywhere, no Wi-Fi required. For users who want true mobility and freedom, one of our unlimited tablet data plans is the best way to stay online wherever you go.

Can I bring my own device when signing up for a tablet plan?

Yes! With Boost Mobile’s BYOD program, you can bring your own tablet and connect it to one of our affordable data plans. As long as your tablet has built-in cellular capabilities, you can enjoy unlimited data for just $20/mo.

Bringing your own device makes it easy to keep the tablet you already love while getting the flexibility and value of a Boost Mobile data plan.
