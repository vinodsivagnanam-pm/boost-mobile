How do I subscribe to Premium Visual Voicemail?

Download the App:

  1. Start by installing the Boost Visual Voicemail app.
  2. Open the Boost Visual Voicemail app and follow the prompts to setup.
  3. Within the app tap Go Premium at the top of the screen and then tap Subscribe to complete your purchase

Note: Premium Visual Voicemail is compatible on Android 12.0 or higher and will require a minimum account balance of $3 to install.

Premium Visual Voicemail Setup:

Within Visual Voicemail, tap the three lines in the left corner → Settings → Preferences to customize your preferences.

Customizing your Themes, Folders and Voicemail:

Themes

  • Tap the three lines in the upper left to open the menu.
  • Go to Settings → Preferences → Display → Themes.
  • Select the theme you'd like to use.

Folders

  • Tap the three lines in the upper left to open the menu.
  • Go to Create New Folder.
  • Enter the name of the new folder.

Voicemail blocking

  • Tap the three lines in the upper left to open the menu.
  • Go to Settings → Voicemail blocking.
  • Add the number you'd like to have blocked.

Compatibility

  • Android: Compatible with Android 12.0 or higher. Requires a minimum account balance of $3 to install.
  • iPhone: No installation needed—this feature is already built into your device.

Already a Visual Voicemail User? When you update to Premium Visual Voicemail you'll have to update your settings.
