Installing your Motorola eSIM

Finish Setting Up Your Motorola:

If you have an eSIM compatible Motorola device, you'll receive a notification on your home screen titled "Finish Cellular Setup". Open this notification and tap “Continue”. When you finish installing your eSIM you will receive a confirmation message that your cellular setup is complete..

If you missed the notification:

You can finish installing your Motorola eSIM by going to Settings → Network & Internet → Mobile Network.

If no eSIM profile is present, a “Download your SIM” prompt appears. Tap Next → Activate. From the “Boost is active” screen, tap Done. Your mobile plan will be automatically downloaded and activate on the eSIM. This step can take up to 5 minutes. Please skip the QR scan step if it pops up during this flow, that step is not necessary for Boost Mobile eSIM activation. Click here for more specific directions on adding an eSIM to your Motorola device.
