SIM Cards & eSIMs

Learn about the differences between SIM cards & eSIMs.

What is a SIM Card?

A SIM card is a tiny chip with all the info your device needs to hook up to Boost Mobile. Just pop it into your phone, and you're set to make calls, text, and surf the web.

Each Boost Mobile SIM Kit has all three sizes (nano, micro, and standard), so your device will have the right fit.

Take a peek at our SIM card installation guide.

What's an eSIM?

An eSIM, or embedded SIM, is a digital SIM that ditches the need for a physical card. You can quickly and easily transfer or get a new cellular plan, all online. Plus, manage multiple eSIMs on one device for dual numbers. It's safer too—no one can swipe it if your phone goes missing.

Learn how to download and install your eSIM.

Can I activate my device via eSIM?

If you have an eSIM capable device, absolutely! Click here for a full list of eSIM supported devices by Boost Mobile.

How long will it take to activate my device?

In most cases, activation just takes a few minutes.

What is a dual SIM?

Dual SIM lets you have two active phone numbers on your device at the same time. For instance, you can use one for business and another for personal use. It's also handy for adding a local data plan when you travel outside the US.

Once you activate your second plan, you can label your plans, set your default number for voice and data and customize preferences between your two phone numbers. Learn how to manage dual SIMs on your device with these step-by-step guides .
