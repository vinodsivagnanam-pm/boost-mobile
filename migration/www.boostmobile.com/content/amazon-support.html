FAQs

How do I unlock my phone?

Unlocking your phone usually takes a few hours, but sometimes a few days so plan ahead. Follow these steps to get your phone unlocked. Once that’s done, we’ll handle the rest.

  • Pay off your phone in your current carrier’s app or website
  • Confirm your phone is unlocked. Check your previous carrier’s unlock policies online.
  • If your phone is paid off but still locked, contact your previous carrier

For more help, visit our device unlock page.

How do I port my number?

You’ll need the following info fro your current carrier to transfer your number to Boost Mobile:

  • Account number
  • Account owner’s name
  • Account billing address
  • Current carrier
  • Number transfer PIN (port out PIN)
  • Phone number

For more help, visit our number transfer guide.

What if my phone isn’t compatible with the SIM?

If your phone’s model number is NOT listed here, then call 833-626-6786 and our support team will send you a new SIM that is compatible!

How do I find my IMEI and ICCID?

To find your ICCID: On the back of the SIM card delivered to you, locate the 20 digit number in the bottom left corner under the ICCID barcode.

To find your IMEI: Call *#06#
