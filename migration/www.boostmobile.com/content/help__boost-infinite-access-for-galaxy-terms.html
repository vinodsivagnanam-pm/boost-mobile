Infinite Access for Galaxy Terms

View Full PDF & Download

Effective as of June 30, 2026, the Infinite Access for Galaxy plan is no longer being offered by Boost Mobile. Existing customers enrolled in this plan prior to June 30, 2026 may continue to enjoy all the benefits, credits, and upgrade privileges of this plan, subject to compliance with the Infinite Access for Galaxy terms and the Boost Mobile Terms and Conditions.

Specific Samsung Galaxy S Series and Z Series models may not be available. Prices vary based on the Samsung Galaxy series model selected. Offer and Offer Terms subject to modification or termination at any time in Boost Mobile’s sole discretion. Taxes on full pre-credit retail price of phone (the “List Price”) due at sale. Monthly $65 fee includes unlimited talk, text and data and an eligible Samsung Galaxy series model (credit value between approximately $380.00 and $1,100.00 depending on the model and specific promotion at the time of your purchase (the “Phone Credit Value”)) and does not include taxes, regulatory fees or surcharges. Samsung Galaxy series models over the Phone Credit Value may result in an additional monthly fee. Available only for qualified customers who finance an eligible Samsung Galaxy series model on the Infinite Access plan; other phones are not eligible. Switching plans may cause you to lose current plan, promotional offers, or feature benefits. Max 10 lines. If customer fails to activate the Infinite Access wireless service plan within 30 days following the date the phone is purchased, Boost Mobile will charge customer full List Price of the purchased Galaxy phone using the payment method on file. Customers using more than 30GB of data will experience speeds reduced to 512kbps for the remainder of the billing cycle. Unlimited data in US only. Coverage not available in some areas; Boost Mobile is not responsible for its’ partners’ networks. Customers are eligible for annual upgrade from Boost Mobile (i.e., each year that they remain on the Infinite Access plan) to a qualifying Galaxy series phone model after making full payment of at least 12 scheduled monthly payments and maintaining active service in good standing (including, without limitation, current in your payments) for such year. Upgrade requires trade-in to Boost Mobile of current Infinite Access plan phone (must be the same IMEI as the phone specified in customer’s finance agreement or, if applicable, replacement phone provided pursuant to a valid Boost Protect claim) in acceptable condition as set forth in the Boost Mobile Terms and Conditions. Customer will receive their Phone Credit Value applied to their eligible new phone upon upgrade. Failure to return current phone will result in customer being charged the entire remaining balance on that phone. 36-Month Finance Agreement: Infinite Access plan requires a 36-month finance agreement with Boost Mobile for the phone included, upgrading ends current financing agreement and any device promotion or deposit reimbursement credits and begins new 36-month financing agreement. The customer’s Phone Credit Value will be applied in equal amounts over a 36-month period depending on current promotion and model of Galaxy series phone customer chooses as long as customer remains active in good standing on the Infinite Access plan. If customer’s Infinite Access plan is canceled, terminated or suspended (including cancellation by customer), credits are forfeited and customer will be immediately responsible for the outstanding balance due on the finance agreement. See finance agreement for full details. Early payment forfeits remaining credits. Customer is not required to upgrade and trade-in their phone every year to participate in the Infinite Access plan.

North America Connect: Enrolled customers receive 5GB of data per month while roaming in Mexico and Canada, unlimited talk, text across North America while roaming, plus unlimited talk and text from the U.S. to Mexico and Canada. Customer must reside in the U.S. and primary usage must occur in the U.S.

Global Talk & Text: Allows enrolled customers to talk and text to more than 190 countries in accordance with the International Long Distance Consumer Agreement (Maximum usage varies by country, click here for limits and for more information about Global Talk & Text.) Offer terms are in addition to, and in no way limitation of, Boost Mobile Terms and Conditions.

Your specific plan terms may vary depending on your date of activation.
