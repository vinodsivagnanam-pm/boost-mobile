Biometric Information

Boost Mobile Biometric Identifiers and Biometric Data/Information Notice

With your consent, Boost Mobile ( “Boost” “we” or “us”) collects, uses and stores your Biometric Identifiers and Biometric Data/Information for fraud detection and prevention purposes.

Use, Storage, and Collection of Biometric Data

Before consumers purchase a device from Boost Mobile, we must verify their identity. To do so, our service provider may scan a copy of your government ID (e.g., a photo of your driver’s license) in tandem with facial recognition technologies as well as other “behavioral biometrics” technologies (e.g., keystroke dynamics, phone movements (e.g. gyroscope and accelerometer), screen swipe and touch/pressure analysis, as well as mouse behavior).

Biometric Authentication (App Login)

If you choose to enable biometric login on the Boost Mobile app, we may facilitate the use of biometric authentication features provided by your device’s operating system, such as Apple Face ID or Touch ID (collectively, “Biometric Login”). When you enable Biometric Login, your device captures and processes biometric identifiers - such as facial geometry or fingerprint data - solely on the device itself. These biometric identifiers are processed and stored within the secure hardware of your device (e.g., Apple’s Secure Enclave) and are not transmitted to, accessed by, or stored on Boost Mobile’s servers. Boost Mobile receives only a success or failure authentication signal from your device’s operating system; Boost Mobile does not receive, possess, or retain any raw biometric templates or biometric identifier data used by the device’s biometric authentication system.

The purpose of Biometric Login is to authenticate your identity and provide secure, convenient access to the Boost Mobile app without requiring manual entry of a password or PIN. By enabling Biometric Login, you consent to the use of your device’s biometric authentication features for this purpose.

Because Boost Mobile does not store biometric identifiers or biometric data used for Biometric Login on its own servers, no biometric data retention schedule applies to Boost Mobile with respect to this feature. Your biometric identifiers remain stored on your device in accordance with the device manufacturer’s security practices and are deleted when you disable the biometric feature, remove the Boost Mobile app, or reset your device. Boost Mobile does not sell, lease, trade, or otherwise disclose biometric identifiers or biometric information collected through Biometric Login to any third party.

Retention, Disclosure, and Protection of Biometric Data

Unless we are legally required to keep your Biometric Data/Information or Biometric Identifiers for a longer period of time, our service provider retains your Biometric Identifiers and Biometric Data/Information until the initial purpose of its collection is complete.

We disclose your Biometric Identifiers and Biometric Data/Information to our service provider for the purposes described herein. We may, however, disclose this such information where required by law (e.g., to law enforcement in response to a valid warrant or subpoena). We do not disclose your Biometric Identifiers and Biometric Data/Information with parties apart from our service provider, or use your Biometric Identifiers and Biometric Data/Information for any other purposes.

Data Protection and Breach Notification

We maintain a variety of safeguards to protect your information. We maintain breach response and notification protocols in the event of a breach of your Biometric Identifiers or Biometric Data/Information.

Your Privacy Rights

Boost Mobile requires your consent to disclose, use, store, and collect, or your Biometric Identifiers and Biometric Data/Information for fraud detection and prevention purposes.
