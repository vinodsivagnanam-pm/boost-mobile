Account Types

Your Account, Your Way

Choose how you pay with options that fit your style. Buy a phone outright, finance it over time, or bring your own device – you're in control.

No Contract

  • Bring your favorite device
  • Pay upfront for what you need. All value, no fluff.
  • Qualified customers can finance devices through Affirm
  • Get a $5 discount with Autopay on the Unlimited, Unlimited+ and Unlimited Premium plans.
  • Special Deals on the newest phones on select plans
  • Add up to 10 lines

Buy Now, Pay Later

  • Multiple financing options
  • Get a $5 discount with Autopay on the Unlimited, Unlimited+ and Unlimited Premium plans*
  • Add up to 10 lines
  • Payment due date will be 15 days after each billing cycle begins. Please note that online orders may require the first month's payment upfront at time of ordering.
  • Itemized bill

Note: Buy Now, Pay Later accounts require Autopay and a soft credit check at account creation. Once you establish a Buy Now, Pay Later account, any lines you add will enjoy the same account experience.

*excludes legacy Boost Infinite plans

All Boost Mobile accounts setup prior to July 17th, 2024 were initially set up as No Contract. All Boost Infinite accounts were initially set up as Buy Now, Pay Later.

If you aren’t sure if you have a Buy Now, Pay Later or No Contract account, please contact Boost Customer Care . Note: No Contract accounts are often referred to as "prepaid" and Buy Now, Pay Later accounts are often referred to as "postpaid".

Why would my account type change from No Contract to Buy Now, Pay Later?

If you finance a device directly through Boost, your account will become a Buy Now, Pay Later account. Note: this does not happen if you choose to finance a purchase through Affirm.

Learn more about device financing options

Does anything change on my account now that there are new plans and account types available?

Not at all. You'll have the option to change to new plans if you'd like, but if you love the plan you have, keep it. You'll continue to enjoy the same great Boost Mobile experience.
