Rich Communication Services (RCS)

What is Rich Communication Services (RCS)?

Rich Communication Services (RCS) creates a more unified and engaging messaging experience across devices with different operating systems. Think about those group chats with iPhones and Androids. RCS adds typing indicators, high-res photo and video sharing, read receipts, and more. It’s replacing old-school SMS and MMS. Don't worry—SMS still has your back if you only have a basic cell connection.

Can Boost Mobile customers use RCS on iPhone?

As long as you have an iPhone XS/XR or later and iOS 18.2, you’re good to go!

How do I update my iPhone to enable RCS?

Easy. Just follow these steps:

  1. Download and install the latest iOS version: Settings → General → Software Update.
  2. Open Settings → General → About. A Carrier Settings Update popup will appear. Update the carrier settings to Boost 61.1 or later.
  3. Restart your iPhone.

How do I turn on/off RCS Messaging?

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Messages.
  2. Under Text Messaging, click RCS Messaging. Toggle on/off RCS Messaging.
