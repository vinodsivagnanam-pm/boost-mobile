The following terms apply to specific products and Services provided by Boost Mobile:

Messaging
Message rates are charged when a message is sent or received, whether read or unread, viewed or unviewed, solicited or unsolicited. Premium and international messages may result in additional charges.

Interactive Directory Assistance
Each use of Interactive Directory Assistance is $2.00 per call.

Accounts with Multiple Lines (a.k.a. “Family Plans”)
The first phone activated on your account will be the primary line for that account. The owner of the primary line is responsible for payment. If the primary line is canceled, another line on the account will become the primary line and/or the family account will be converted to individual accounts/lines at our discretion.
