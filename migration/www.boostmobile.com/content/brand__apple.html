FAQs

When does the new iPhone come out?

Apple announced its new high-end iPhone 18 lineup at its September 9th event, with an official release date of Friday, September 18th for the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max.

What is the newest iPhone?

Apple's latest releases are the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and the new foldable iPhone Duo.

A quick breakdown of the new iPhone models:

  • iPhone 18 Pro & 18 Pro Max – These premium options feature the new A20 Pro chip and a smaller Dynamic Island. They also offer up to 43 hours of video playback.
  • iPhone Duo – This is Apple's first foldable phone. It features a 5.4-inch outer panel, a 7.6-inch inner display, and Touch ID instead of Face ID.
How much does the new iPhone 18 Pro cost?
  • iPhone 18 Pro – Starts at $1,199
  • iPhone 18 Pro Max – This model starts at $1,299. The top-tier 2TB variant is priced at $2,399.
