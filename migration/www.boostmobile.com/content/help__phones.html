What devices are available from Boost?

Boost Mobile has you covered with the latest and greatest devices from top brands like Apple, Samsung, Motorola, Google and more. Whether you're looking for cutting-edge features or simple affordability, you'll find it here.

Shop our phones, tablets and smartwatches and find your perfect match today.

Can I bring my own phone to Boost?

Yes. Most popular, unlocked phones can be activated on Boost Mobile with ease. Keep using the phone you love while enjoying all the benefits of Boost's plans and services. For details, check out our bring your own phone guide. Join Boost today and keep the device you love!

What financing options do you offer?

You can finance your device with Affirm or directly through Boost Mobile, depending on the specific device and plan you choose. Your financing options will be displayed during checkout.

Can I upgrade my device?

Of course! At Boost, we give you the flexibility to upgrade your phone whenever you're ready. Whether you're eyeing the latest model or just want something new, we've got you covered. For all the details on how to upgrade your device, check out our upgrade guide. It's quick, easy, and designed to keep you connected with the best devices.

Can I bring my own tablet or watch to Boost Mobile?

Yes, Boost Mobile supports iPad 8th Generation and later and Apple Watch Series 6 and later (cellular models). We also support Android tablets and Samsung Galaxy Watch7/ Watch FE/ Watch Ultra and later.

You can double check our tablet and watch confirmation pages to make sure your device is compatible.
