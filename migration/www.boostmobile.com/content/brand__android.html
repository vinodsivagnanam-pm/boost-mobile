What Android version do I have?

To find out which version of Android your device is currently running:

  • Open your Settings app
  • Scroll down to ‘About phone’ (sometimes called System or Device information)
  • Select ‘Software information’
  • Look for ‘Android version’

The exact steps may vary slightly depending on your phone brand (Samsung, Google, Motorola, etc.), but every Android device has this information built in. Newer phones, such as the Galaxy S25 Ultra, come with Android 15 out of the box, while devices like Google’s Pixel 10 may already be running on the new Android 16. Knowing your version helps you understand what features, security patches, and apps your device supports.

How to upgrade to Android 16

To upgrade your phone to Android 16, make sure your device is eligible and has enough storage space available. The latest version of Android is currently only available for Google devices, but is expected to be released for all Android phones by the end of 2025.

If your device is eligible:

  • Connect to Wi-Fi and ensure your phone has at least 50% battery.
  • Go to Settings > System > Software Update.
  • Tap Check for Updates.
  • If Android 16 is available, select Download and Install.

Most of the time, the update to Android 16 will happen automatically when the software is available for your specific device.
