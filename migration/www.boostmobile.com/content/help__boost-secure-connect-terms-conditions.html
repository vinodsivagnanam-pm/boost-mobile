What information does Secure Connect access or collect and why?

Operational and Statistical Data. The following data may be collected and stored when it is required to operate the service:

  • Application ID - An anonymous ID assigned to the application at installation.
  • Account Information - Carrier billing SubscriberID and/or Phone Number may be used to establish and maintain the subscription status for this service.
  • Device Data - Unique device identifiers that can be traced back to you are not collected.
  • Connection Data - Network data usage (bytes transferred) for the sole purpose of internal operational and performance monitoring.
  • Anonymous Aggregate Usage Statistics Data - basic statistical information for customer support and service quality maintenance, this strictly excludes all sensitive activity logs or personally identifiable information.
  • Crash Logs - Crash log information regarding your device’s use of the application for troubleshooting and to improve Mobolize’s application. Crash logs do not contain your IP addresses or other personal information.
  • Problem Reports - Your name and email address are collected when you report a problem through the application. When you provide feedback, you can choose whether to share logs from the app. Logs can include information about your device, domains you have visited, Wi-Fi network SSID, and IP addresses. This information is used for troubleshooting and to improve the Secure Connect application. Your name, email address and these logs, if you share them, will be deleted 90 days from the date of collection.

Permissions. Certain permissions may be requested to enable the service, including:

  • VPN Profile and/or Service - Depending on which features you have enabled in the application this may include DNS and Data encryption, and transmission of that encrypted data, to Mobolize’s secure network to provide you data privacy and protection services.
  • Notifications - To communicate to you through the application for security alerts and subscription-based functions only.
  • Phone State (Android only) - To enable account authentication and subscription status for this service. Your phone # is never shared with 3rd parties.
  • Location (Android only) - To detect the name (SSID) of Wi-Fi networks, which allows the application to automatically protect the specific Wi-Fi networks chosen by you and to allow you to control which Wi-Fi networks you are warned about. No actual location information is collected or shared outside of the app on your device.

Third Party Data Sharing

This application does not sell your personal information to third parties.
