Installing your Google Pixel eSIM

Finish Setting Up Cellular:

Once your line is activated, you'll receive a notification on your home screen titled Boost Cellular Plan Ready. Open this notification and tap Continue. When your eSIM is installed you’ll receive a confirmation message that your activation is complete.

If you can’t find, or accidentally remove the notification from your Home Screen, you can recover your eSIM profile by going to your Settings → Network & Internet → tap SIMs → Download a new eSIM. Your mobile plan will be automatically downloaded and activate on the eSIM. Click here for more specific directions on adding an eSIM to your Google Pixel device.
