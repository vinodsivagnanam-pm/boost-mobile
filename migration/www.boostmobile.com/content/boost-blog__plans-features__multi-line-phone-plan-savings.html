Understanding Multi-Line & Family Phone Plans

A multi-line phone plan allows you to connect multiple devices under one mobile plan, usually at a reduced cost per line compared to buying separate single-line plans. With Boost, we offer great discounts when you add more lines to your plan. Visit our multi-line plans page to see current offers and start saving today!

Whether you call them family phone plans or group plans, the concept is the same: shared benefits and reduced price per line for everyone involved.
