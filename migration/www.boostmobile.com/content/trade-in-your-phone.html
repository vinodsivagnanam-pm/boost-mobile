How to Trade In Your Device

1

Tell us about your device

Choose your new phone, select the trade-in option, enter your current phone's IMEI, and answer a few questions about its condition to get an offer.

2

Clear your data

Be sure to back up your content, sign out of iCloud (iOS), and factory reset your device before sending it to us.

3

Mail your device

We’ll send you a trade-in kit. Be sure to mail your old device to us in the smaller box included in the kit within 30 days.

4

Receive credit

We’ll apply the credit to your Boost account once we receive and inspect your device. Use it to cover your monthly service costs.

Got Trade-In Questions? We Have Answers.

Can I trade in any device?

We’ll use your phone’s IMEI number and current condition to determine if your device is eligible for trade-in. Based on your answers, we’ll provide an estimate of your trade-in value. After you send us your phone, we’ll send you a final trade-in value after validating its current condition.

Can I trade in my current Boost Mobile phone?

Yes. As long as you are not currently financing your Boost Mobile phone and haven't received a special device discount in the last nine months, you can trade in your current device.

Can I trade in multiple devices?

You can trade in one device for each new phone you purchase.

What happens if I change my mind?

If you decide you don’t want to accept the estimated offer, you can remove the trade-in from your cart at any time before completing your purchase. Once your phone is sent in, the trade-in is final.

Does my phone need to be in perfect condition for a trade-in?

No, your trade-in value will be adjusted based on its condition. We will reject a trade-in if the device does not turn on, or if Find My iPhone and/or Activation Lock are active. Additionally, your trade-in credit may be reduced if the phone's condition is worse than you initially described.
