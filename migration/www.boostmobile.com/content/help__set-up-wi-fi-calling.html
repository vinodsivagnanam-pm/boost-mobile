Set up Wi-Fi Calling

Let's walk you through how to set it up on both Apple and Android devices.

Apple

  1. Go to Settings on your Home screen.
  2. Tap Cellular → Wi-Fi Calling.
  3. Toggle Wi-Fi Calling on This iPhone to On (green color).

Android

  1. Go to Settings in your App drawer.
  2. Tap Connections → Wi-Fi Calling.
  3. Toggle Wi-Fi Calling to ON.

NOTE

For both Android and iPhone, you might also be asked to confirm your address for emergency services the first time you use Wi-Fi calling.
