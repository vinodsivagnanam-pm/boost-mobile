Top 5 iOS 17 Features

● Interactive widgets: iOS 17 introduces interactive widgets, which allow you to interact with elements of the widget directly from the home screen. For example, you can tap a widget to check off an item on your to-do list or play a song from your music library.

● StandBy Mode: StandBy mode is a new power-saving feature that puts your phone into a sleep-state when you're not using it for a longer period of time. This can help to extend your phone’s battery life significantly.

● Live Voicemail: You can now preview and transcribe your voicemail messages before listening to them. There is an additional feature that will have unknown callers go directly to Live Voicemail when turning this feature on.

● SharePlay: This feature allows everyone to control music in the car, so everyone can contribute to what is playing.

● Screen Distance for eye health: This feature helps you to stay at a healthy distance from your iPhone screen to improve overall eye health.
