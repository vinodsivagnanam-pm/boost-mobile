WHAT IS DATA ROAMING?

By Boost Mobile

Published July 21, 2025

If you’ve ever taken your phone on a trip and wondered, “What is data roaming and why does it matter?” you’re not alone. Data roaming happens when your mobile device connects to a network outside of your carrier’s primary coverage area. This is common when you travel, whether you’re crossing state lines or jetting off overseas. Roaming keeps you connected to data, calls, and texts when you’re outside your usual network.

For travelers, data roaming is an essential feature, but it can also be confusing. This article breaks down everything from domestic vs. international roaming to how data roaming works on an iPhone. You’ll also learn about international calling and why prepaid plans like Boost Mobile’s make managing data roaming simpler and more affordable.

When Does Data Roaming Happen?

Domestic vs. International Roaming

Data roaming can occur both domestically and internationally. Domestic roaming happens when your phone connects to another carrier’s network within the same country. This might be because you’re in a rural area where your primary provider doesn’t have towers, so it partners with another network to keep you covered.

International roaming, on the other hand, kicks in when you travel abroad. For example, if you’re in Mexico or Europe, your phone connects to a local carrier through agreements made with your home provider. This allows you to use your data, make calls, and send texts almost anywhere.

What Is iPhone Data Roaming?

What Is iPhone Data Roaming?

Wondering specifically, “What is iPhone data roaming?” It’s the same concept, but controlled within your iPhone’s settings. Both iPhone and Android devices let you toggle data roaming on or off. On an iPhone, go to Settings > Cellular > Cellular Data Options > Data Roaming.

Turning data roaming off prevents your phone from using mobile data when you’re outside your carrier’s main network. This can help you avoid unexpected charges. On Android, you’ll find a similar option under Settings > Network & Internet > Mobile Network > Roaming.

What Is International Calling?

How International Calling Works

International calling allows you to dial a number outside your home country. There are two main ways to do this: through traditional cellular networks or over the internet using VoIP (Voice over Internet Protocol) services like WhatsApp or Skype.

Traditional international calling uses cellular towers and usually costs more. VoIP uses your internet connection, so it might be cheaper or even free, but it depends on your data or Wi-Fi availability.

Additional Questions on Data Roaming

Do I want data roaming on or off?

This is one of the most common questions people ask. Keeping data roaming off when you’re traveling abroad is often recommended unless you have a plan that covers roaming. This way, you can avoid surprise charges. If you’ve added a global roaming or international data package through your provider, turning data roaming on lets you use data as you would at home, within your plan limits.

Do I get charged for data roaming?

Yes—typically, data roaming involves extra fees, especially when you’re outside your carrier’s standard coverage area. These charges depend on your carrier and whether you’ve added an international or roaming package. With Boost, you can control costs by choosing a plan or Add-On that covers roaming.

Is data roaming free with unlimited data?

Unlimited data plans generally cover use within your carrier’s domestic network. Data roaming—especially internationally—is usually not included, even if you have unlimited data at home. Always check your plan details to see what roaming data is covered.

What is the difference between cellular data and data roaming?

Cellular data refers to the data your phone uses to connect to the internet through your carrier’s network. Data roaming is when your phone uses another network outside of your carrier’s main coverage area—like when you’re traveling domestically in a partner network zone or internationally on a foreign carrier.

Can I use Boost internationally?

Yes. Boost offers global roaming options and international calling Add-Ons so you can use your phone while traveling. Check out Boost’s international services to see available countries and rates.

Will I be charged for incoming calls overseas?

Generally, yes. When you’re roaming internationally, many carriers—including Boost—apply charges to both outgoing and incoming calls. It’s a good idea to review the rates before you travel so you’re not caught off guard.
