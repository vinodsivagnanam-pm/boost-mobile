Installing your iPhone eSIM

Finish Setting Up Cellular:

Heads up! Make sure your iOS software is up-to-date before installing your new eSIM

If you have an eSIM compatible iPhone, you'll receive a notification on your home screen titled Finish Cellular Setup once your line is activated. Open this notification to install your Cellular Plan and tap Continue. When your eSIM is installed you’ll receive a confirmation message that your activation is complete.

If you can’t find, or accidentally remove the notification from your Home Screen, you can recover your eSIM profile by going to Settings → Cellular → Set Up Cellular (or add eSIM). Follow the prompts to finish setting up your cellular service.
