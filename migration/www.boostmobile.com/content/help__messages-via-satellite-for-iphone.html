Messages via Satellite for iPhone

What is Messages via satellite on iPhone?

Starting in iOS 18, Messages via satellite lets you stay connected with friends and family when you’re off the grid. No cellular or Wi-Fi connection? No problem - you can securely send texts, emojis, and Tapbacks over iMessage and SMS. Reminder: you need to be outside with a clear view of the sky.

Is Messages via satellite free?

Yes! Messages via satellite is free for two years after the activation of an iPhone 14 or later (all models).

Where is Messages via satellite available?

Messages via satellite is currently available in the U.S. and Canada.

What’s different about satellite messaging compared to regular texting?

Great question! Unlike mobile networks that rely on local cell towers for instant communication, satellite messaging only needs a direct line of sight to the satellite to send and receive messages.

Which devices support satellite messaging?

iPhone 14 and later models running iOS 18. If you’ve got one of these, you’re set to send messages from the great outdoors!
