Add Lines

Adding a New Line:

Add lines to your account by signing in and selecting Add A Line on the web or by tapping Add New Lines to My Account in the Boost Mobile app.

Need help? Contact Boost Customer Care.

Note: Some plans can only be added in-store.

How many lines can I have on my account?

You can have up to 10 lines on your account.

Do the lines on my account need to share data?

Nope! Each line has its own.

Can I have different plans on the same account?

We're flexible. Many of our plans can be mixed and matched with other plans on the same account.
