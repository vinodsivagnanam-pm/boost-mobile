Which budget phone has the best camera?

If photography is what matters most to you, the Pixel 10 is the clear winner. Thanks to its 48MP main lens, ultrawide and telephoto options, and Google AI-powered photography, it packs a cinematic punch in the budget smartphone department.

The iPhone 16e and Galaxy S25 FE are also stand out choices, with night modes, imaginative portrait effects, and versatile zoom capabilities. For most casual photographers, these budget phones produce photos nearly indistinguishable from premium models.

Yes, many 2026 budget phones are designed to handle streaming and moderate gaming. Devices like the iPhone 16e, Galaxy S25 FE, and Pixel 10 feature powerful processors (A17, Exynos, Tensor G5), smooth displays, and ample memory.

What features are important in a budget phone?

When choosing the perfect budget phone, look for:

  • Performance: A fast processor and sufficient RAM for smooth multitasking

  • Battery life: Long-lasting and preferably fast or wireless charging

  • Display: Bright, sharp, and high refresh rate for smooth visuals

  • Camera: Versatile lenses and AI enhancements for good photos

  • Software updates: Phones that get regular updates last longer

  • Connectivity: 5G support, Wi-Fi, and reliable network compatibility
