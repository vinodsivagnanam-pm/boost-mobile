Infinite Access Plans

Important Update about Infinite Access Plans

On June 30, 2026, Infinite Access plans are no longer available for purchase on new lines of service. But if you’re already an existing customer with an Infinite Access plan, your plan, your benefits, and your yearly phone upgrades aren't going anywhere.

Plan Details for Existing Customers

Infinite Access for iPhone - $65/mo.

  • 30GB of Premium Data
  • New iPhone Included
  • Global Talk & Text - Enjoy talk and text from the U.S. to over 190 destinations.
  • North America Connect - Stay in touch while traveling throughout North America.
  • Annual Upgrades
  • $5/mo autopay discount included

Infinite Access for Galaxy - $65/mo.

  • 30GB of Premium Data
  • New Galaxy S or Z Series Included
  • Global Talk & Text - Enjoy talk and text from the U.S. to over 190 destinations.
  • North America Connect - Stay in touch while traveling throughout North America.
  • Annual Upgrades
  • $5/mo autopay discount included

See Infinite Access for iPhone or Galaxy terms for full details.

I'm an existing customer. What happens to my Infinite Access plan after June 30, 2026?

Absolutely nothing. Your plan is grandfathered in. You keep your unlimited wireless, your monthly payment, and your ability to score the newest phone every single year.

What device models are available for existing customers doing their annual upgrade?

Each year, you’ll have access to the latest iPhone or Galaxy S or Z Series model. You also have the flexibility to upgrade to larger storage capacity and the ability to get other available models for an additional monthly charge.

How do I get the latest iPhone or Galaxy every year?

Here’s the deal: make 12 straight monthly payments, and you can swap your current phone for the latest model. When you’re ready, hit up the Boost Mobile app to request your new device. We’ll ship it right to your door, complete with a box and shipping label for your trade-in. We’ll check your old phone for any damage once we get it.

No pressure to upgrade after 12 months. If you’re still loving your phone, keep it. It’ll be all yours after 36 monthly payments.

Boost your Infinite Access Plan with Custom Add-Ons

North America Connect and Global Talk & Text are included in your Infinite Access plan. Now, take it up a notch and tailor your plan with adaptable Add-Ons, perfectly suited to your unique lifestyle.

You can enable Add-Ons at any time in your account online, in the Boost Mobile app or by contacting Boost Customer Care.

How do I protect my new phone?

Get peace of mind with Boost Protect and rest easy knowing your phone is protected. It covers you in the event of phone loss, theft, accidental damage and out-of-warranty mechanical or electrical breakdown. Contact us to add Boost Protect to your plan today.

Are there any hidden fees?

Nope, no hidden fees here. Apart from taxes and any local surcharges, what you see is what you get. Unlike most carriers, Boost Mobile offers straightforward pricing, so you know exactly what you're being billed for.
