If you do not wish to receive telemarketing communications from Boost Mobile, call us to place your information on our Do Not Contact List, contact us via my.boostmobile.com, use the Do Not Contact Request form online, or by calling us at (833) 502-6678. Please include your name, address, email address, and all telephone numbers you want included on our Do Not Contact List when you make your request. If your contact information changes, please notify us of your new name, address, email address, and/or telephone number(s) to remain on our Do Not Contact List accurately. We will remove your name and information from our active lists as soon as possible, but not more than ten (10) days after you ask us to do so.
