Family Plan Discount:
 Unlimited+ Plan ($50/mo) and Unlimited Premium Plan ($60/mo) pricing is available with eligible activations. Multi-line savings apply when two or more eligible lines, all on Unlimited+ and/or Unlimited Premium plans, are active on the same account. Savings are applied at the account level as follows: $20/month off the 2nd line, $20/month off the 3rd line, $40/month off the 4th line, and $20/month off each additional line from the 5th through the 10th line. Savings are automatically applied at checkout when eligibility requirements are met and will end if eligibility requirements are no longer met.
