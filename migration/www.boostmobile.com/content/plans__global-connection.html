More About Our Global Connection Plan

What is Global Talk & Text?

Global Talk & Text is the premier way to stay connected across the globe with all your friends and family, no matter where they are.

Features Included in the Global Connection plan:

  • Unlimited texting to 191+ countries

  • Unlimited calling to 100+ countries

Check out all the countries included with Global Talk & Text

What is Global Roaming?

Global Roaming is a feature built into your plan that lets you use your phone overseas without the worry of unexpected roaming fees.

This feature includes:

  • Talk and Text: Coverage within your travel destination and back to the U.S. 90+ countries included around the world

  • International data: 1GB of data per month. If you need more, you can add up to 9 extra gigabytes for $10/GB (any added data is valid for 30 days)

Can I bring my own phone to the Global Connection plan?

Yes! You can easily switch to the Global Connection plan with your current unlocked, compatible device. Keeping your own phone means there is no need to purchase a new device or sign a financing contract.
