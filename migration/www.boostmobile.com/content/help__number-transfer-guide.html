Keep your number

You’ll need the following info from your previous carrier to transfer your number to Boost Mobile

  1. Account number
  2. Account owner's name
  3. Account billing address
  4. Number transfer PIN (port-out PIN)

Pro-Tip: Numbers 1-3 in the list above are typically found on your bill.
