FAQs

How do I join Boost CARES?

Step 1: Click here to opt into the program

Step 2: Continue using boostmobile.com to select and purchase ANY of our great rate plans

Step 3: Activate your great service!

Step 4: Your participation in the program is now complete! Please look for emails from us that will contain two things:

  • A monthly summary of your accrued contributions
  • Instructions on how to redeem your digital credits for use in the Charity On Top Foundation’s charity marketplace
What does Boost CARES cost me?

There are zero extra costs to you or the nonprofits. As long as you pay your monthly bill, we’ll set aside 5% of each eligible wireless phone line (Excluding device charges, taxes, and fees) each month. In the following month after a regular calendar quarter, Boost will send a unique code for you to access your digital credits for use in the Charity On Top digital marketplace featuring nearly 2 million causes.

What Boost Mobile plans are eligible to participate?

All Boost Mobile plans from boostmobile.com are eligible. Any purchases made outside of boostmobile.com are ineligible at this time.

How do I receive my digital credits and select a cause?

Your contribution is calculated at the end of each quarter, and you will receive a digital code to access your credits on Charity On Top Foundation’s charity marketplace to the primary email address on your Boost Mobile account. This e-mail should arrive approximately 30 days following the end of the quarter close and you will see a Contribution Amount that you can use (The minimum accrual amount required for a digital code is $5. If you don’t exceed this minimum, then your accrual is carried over to the next quarter.). You will have 90 days to make a selection. If you don’t use any or all of your digital credits within this time period, then Boost will credit these to the Charity On Top Foundation’s General Fund.

What happens if I cancel my service?

If a participating customer leaves or disconnects any wireless phone line at any time, then they will automatically surrender their earned Contribution Amount that has not already been distributed. Surrendered Contribution Amounts will be donated directly to Charity On Top’s General Donation Fund.

How do I know if I’m eligible?

Eligibility for Boost CARES is determined by the following:

  • Opt-in to the program: All customers must share their info via this form above to participate in this program

  • Order Status: New wireless phone lines ordered through boostmobile.com

  • Order type: Wireless phone lines only (Excludes: Infinite Access, Free Year of Service, Tablets, Watches)

  • State of Residence: The program is currently not available if you reside in one of the following states: Alabama (AL), Hawaii (HI), Massachusetts (MA).

  • Account Status: Customer accounts whose bills are not current or settled, including suspended account

Note: If you’ve opted in, we’ll notify you via e-mail if you’re considered ineligible for any and all of the reasons listed above.

How do I find my cause in the Charity On Top Platform?

You can find your cause by typing in the name of the organization in the search bar on https://charityontop.org/charities.php. Many names may appear, so you can also use the tax-ID number to find your organization directly. To find the organization's tax-ID, please use the IRS tax exempt organization search tool: https://apps.irs.gov/app/eos/.
