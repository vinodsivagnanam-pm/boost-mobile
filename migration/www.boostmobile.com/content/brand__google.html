Does the Google Pixel have AI features?

Yes! Google began introducing AI-powered features with the Pixel 2, which launched tools like Google Lens integration and Active Edge. Since then, every new Pixel has expanded on AI-powered features, adding innovations such as Call Assist, Magic Eraser, Photo Unblur, and adaptive battery optimization.

With each generation, Google continues to innovate, making Pixel phones smarter and faster in regards to AI capabilities, introducing apps and features into both hardware and software. Google’s latest release, the Pixel 10 Pro Fold, features groundbreaking AI capabilities, like Live Translate which can translate a phone call between people speaking different languages!

Is the Google Pixel an Android device?

Yes! All Google Pixel phones run Android, Google’s own mobile operating system. In fact, Pixel devices often get the latest software updates, security patches, and AI features before any other Android phone.

Whether it’s the Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, or older models, every Pixel combines Google’s software innovations with advanced hardware for a seamless Android experience.

Does the Pixel 10 have vapor chamber cooling?

The base model of the Google Pixel 10 relies on a graphite thermal cooling solution rather than the vapor chamber cooling. While it’s effective for everyday use, it doesn’t manage heat as efficiently as the Pro models during extended gaming or heavy multitasking.

However, the new Tensor G5 chip in the Pixel 10 is designed to help offset this. The chip is designed to run efficiently and keep heat levels low, meaning most users will rarely notice thermal issues in daily use. The Pixel 10 delivers smooth performance and AI-powered features, even without the vapor chamber, making it a strong, reliable option for Boost Mobile customers who want a high-performance Android device.

For those who want the best cooling performance on the market, the Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold are equipped with Google’s vapor chamber cooling system. This advanced setup helps these devices handle prolonged heavy usage, including gaming, video editing, and other resource-intensive tasks, while keeping temperatures lower and performance consistent. Together with the Tensor G5 chip, the Pro models offer a premium experience for users who demand the very best in power, AI, and thermal efficiency.
