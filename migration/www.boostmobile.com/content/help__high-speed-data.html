Get more high-speed data when you need it. Add additional high-speed data in your account online, in the Boost Mobile app or by contacting Boost Customer Care.

Recurring Data Packs

Recurring Data Packs become a part of your monthly payment and will automatically renew until you cancel.

  • 10GB of Premium High-Speed Data - $10/mo
  • Additional data packs available on select plans

One-Time Data Packs

One-time Data Packs expire 30 days after purchase or when the allotment is depleted. They are separate from your monthly plan and will be removed after 30 days.

  • $5 - 2GB of Premium High-Speed Data (available on select plans)
  • $10 - 10GB of Premium High-Speed Data (available on select plans)

Can I use my additional high-speed data with my Data Hotspot Add-On?

You sure can. If you have the Data Hotspot Add-On, any high-speed data you add is accessible by your Hotspot feature, as well.
