Billing and Payments

Learn about how and when we bill you for your service.

Where can I pay my bill?

For accounts enrolled in Autopay, you can view your due date in your account online, in the app or at a Boost Mobile store. Your payment will be processed on the day it is due.

For accounts not enrolled in AutoPay, payments can be made at a Boost Mobile store, in your account online, in the app or by contacting Boost Customer Care.

Assisted payments are subject to a $4 convenience fee.

When is my bill or payment due?

You can view your bill's due date directly on the home dashboard in your account online or in the Boost Mobile app.

How do I see my billing information?

You can check your billing or payment information in your account online or in the Boost Mobile app.

Note: If you financed your purchase through Affirm, your payments will be handled by Affirm. Learn more about how to make your device payments through Affirm.

Can I make a payment in advance?

Absolutely. Payments made in advance will show as a credit on your account and automatically apply at your next payment due date.

Note: Making a payment early does not replenish monthly data allotments.

How do I enroll in AutoPay?

On the web

  1. Go to Settings → Payment Method → Enable AutoPay.
  2. Add a credit or debit card under the Add Credit or Debit Card section.

Boost Mobile app

  1. Go to Settings → Account → Payment Methods → Enable AutoPay.
  2. Add any credit card under the Add Payment Method section.

How do I update AutoPay?

On the web

  1. Go to Settings → Payment Method.
  2. You'll see your saved payment method at the top of the screen. You can add a credit or debit card under the Add Credit or Debit Card section.

Boost Mobile app

  1. Select Settings → Account → Payment Methods.
  2. You'll see your saved payment method at the top of the screen. You can add any credit card under the Add Payment Method section.

How do I turn off AutoPay?

On the web

  1. Go to Settings → Payment Method → toggle AutoPay to the off position.

Boost Mobile app

  1. Go to Settings → Account → Payment Methods → toggle AutoPay to the off position

Note: some account types and plan offers require autopay to remain on.

Which credit cards does Boost Mobile accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover - Please note that we may not be able to process cards from all financial institutions. We do not currently accept prepaid debit or credit cards on boostmobile.com.

If your card isn't working, please contact your card issuer or try using another payment method.

Does Boost Mobile accept Apple Pay and Google Pay?

Absolutely. You can use Apple Pay or Google Pay at checkout on boostmobile.com. You can also use either to make payments or set up AutoPay in the Boost Mobile app or in your account on the web.

Can I make a payment for a friend or family member?

Absolutely. You can make a guest payment for free by calling Boost Customer Care and using our automated system. You just need the person’s phone number and the exact amount you want to pay. To protect our members' privacy, we won’t disclose their account balance, so make sure you have that info before you call.

Note: If you choose to have an agent process the payment for you, a $4 convenience fee will apply.

Can a payment for Boost Mobile service be refunded?

Once a payment is applied to an account, it is not refundable, transferrable, or exchangeable and it has no surrender value.

Need more help? Stop by a Boost Mobile store or contact Boost Customer Care.
