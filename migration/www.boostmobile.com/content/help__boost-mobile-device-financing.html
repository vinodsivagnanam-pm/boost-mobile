0% Financing with Boost Mobile

Boost Mobile offers 36-month 0% APR financing on select Apple devices, including iPads and Apple Watches. We also offer 0% financing on phones purchased by existing customers with an Infinite Access Plan. Don’t worry if your credit isn’t perfect - we offer additional financing options with down payments that can lower your monthly payment.

Note: Device taxes will be applied at checkout and autopay is required.

Do I have to run credit to be approved for device financing?

Yes. We run a soft credit check that won't affect your credit score. And you can rest easy, we will never sell your information.

Can I pay off my device before the end of the device financing agreement?

Yes, and we make it easy. Pay your device off early by contacting Boost Customer Care.

Will my device be billed with my monthly service?

Yes, customers who finance directly with Boost Mobile will see their monthly device charges appear on their regular monthly bill.

What happens if I cancel my Boost Mobile service before my device is paid off?

If you financed your device directly with Boost Mobile, your payment method on file will be charged in full for the remaining balance of the device.

What happens after I make my last phone device payment?

Your device is now paid off and you’re free to unlock it. No more device charges for this phone on your bill!
