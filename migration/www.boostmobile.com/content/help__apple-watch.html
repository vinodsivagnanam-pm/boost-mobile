Is my current Apple Watch compatible with Boost Mobile?

Boost Mobile supports Apple Watch Series 6 and later. (cellular models)

Can I finance an Apple Watch?

Yes, you can finance an Apple Watch directly through Boost Mobile. However, financing an Apple Watch with Affirm is not available at this time.

How does my account change if I add an Apple Watch plan?

If you are currently on a No Contract account, we’ll switch you to a Buy Now, Pay Later account. Buy Now Pay Later accounts:

  • Receive a monthly itemized bill
  • Require Autopay
  • Have a payment due date 15 days after your billing cycle begins

If you already have a Buy Now, Pay Later Account, nothing changes.

How do I activate my Apple Watch?

Before you begin, make sure you update your iPhone and Apple Watch to the latest versions of iOS.

Boost Mobile app

  1. Go to Settings → Device Setup → Activate.
  2. Select the iPhone you will use to activate your Apple Watch.
  3. Confirm your iPhone and Apple Watch software are updated to the latest OS. Next, you’ll open the Watch App on your iPhone to finish your watch and cellular setup.

On the web


1. Log into your Boost Mobile account → Activate. Under Device Setup, click Set Up.
2. Select the iPhone you will use to activate your Apple Watch. Note: Your Apple Watch will be paired with the iPhone you select.
3. Verify both your iPhone and Apple Watch are updated to the latest versions of iOS.
4. Open the Apple Watch app and follow the instructions to complete your activation.

In the Watch App on your iPhone


1. Turn your Apple Watch on and put it on your wrist. Hold your Apple Watch close to your iPhone. When you see the Use your iPhone to set up this Apple Watch message, tap Continue.
2. Hold your iPhone over the animation to pair your Apple Watch.
3. Follow the on screen instructions in the Watch App on your iPhone to setup your watch and cellular plan.

How is setting up a cellular plan for my Apple Watch different than pairing it with my phone?

If your Apple Watch has a cellular plan, it gets its own connection to the Boost Mobile Network. You can use data and apps or make calls without being near your iPhone or connected to Wi-Fi.

Without a cellular plan, you can still access all of these features. Just remember, your watch will need to stay connected to your phone via Bluetooth.

Can I set up a cellular plan on my Apple Watch and use the same phone number from my iPhone?

Absolutely! When you are setting up your Apple Watch, make sure you choose Set up for Myself. Your watch will use the phone number of the iPhone it’s paired with to access services. You’ll be able to make and receive calls, exchange messages, share your location and use cellular data from your watch.

Can I set up a cellular plan on my Apple Watch and use a different phone number not associated with my iPhone?

Of course! When you are setting up your Apple Watch, make sure you choose Set up for a Family Member. Your Apple Watch will not use the phone number of your paired iPhone and will instead use its own number. It will be able to make and receive calls, exchange messages and use data all with its own cellular plan. This setup, known as Apple Watch For Your Kids, lets you manage the settings of an Apple Watch for a family member or child who does not have their own iPhone.
