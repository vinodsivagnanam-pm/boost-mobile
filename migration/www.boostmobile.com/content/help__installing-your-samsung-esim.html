Installing your Samsung eSIM

Finish Setting Up Cellular:

If you have an eSIM compatible Samsung device, you'll receive a notification on your home screen titled Boost Cellular Plan Ready once your line is activated. Open this notification and tap Continue. When your eSIM is installed you’ll receive a confirmation message that your activation is complete.

If you can’t find, or accidentally remove the notification from your Home Screen, you can recover your eSIM profile by going to your Settings → Connections → SIM card manager → Add mobile plan. Your mobile plan will be automatically downloaded and activate on the eSIM. Click here for more specific directions on adding an eSIM to your Samsung device.
