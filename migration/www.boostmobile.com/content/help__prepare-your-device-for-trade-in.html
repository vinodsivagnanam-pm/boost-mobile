Prepare Your Device for Trade-In

Step 1: Prepare your devices

For iPhone:

  • Back up your device.
  • Turn off Stolen Device Protection on your old iPhone.
  • Follow these steps to disable Find My iPhone (FMiP) on your old iPhone. To turn off FMiP remotely, click here.
  • Turn on your new device and place it near the old device.
  • Follow the on screen instructions to complete setup and data transfer.

For Android:

  • Back up your device.
  • Turn on your new device and place it near the old device.
  • Follow the on screen instructions to complete setup and data transfer.
  • Make sure to turn off device protection features. See how to disable device protection features for Android, Pixel, and Samsung.

Step 2: Activate new device

  • Connect your new device to Wi-Fi.
  • Download the Boost Mobile app.
  • Launch the Boost Mobile app and once in the App, tap Settings → Device Setup → Manage → Change Phone.
  • Follow the prompts to activate.

Step 3: Reset your old phone, Pack it up and Ship it out

  • Factory Reset your old device: Follow these instructions to factory reset your iPhone, Galaxy, Motorola or Pixel. Note: Please ensure you erase all content and data from your old device.
  • After powering off your old device, securely wrap it in the provided bubble wrap and place it in the provided box. (One device per box).
  • Close and tape the package(s) securely to avoid any damage during transit.
  • Ship your package(s) via USPS. You can drop it in your outgoing mail, visit the nearest USPS location, or schedule a pickup online at usps.com/pickup.
