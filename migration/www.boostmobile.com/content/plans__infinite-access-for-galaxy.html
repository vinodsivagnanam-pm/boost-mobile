FAQs

Will the Unlimited plan really be $25 for life?

Yes! Paired with our $5/month discount with AutoPay and as long as you never pause or cancel your service, you’ll never have to pay more than $25 for your Unlimited plan. Never worry about hidden fees or overages and enjoy unlimited talk, text and data* for life.

*After 30GB, customers may experience slower speeds

What’s the most affordable unlimited plan from Boost Mobile?

Boost Mobile’s most affordable phone plan is the $25 Forever Unlimited plan. You’ll get unlimited talk, text and data with reliable 5G coverage.

Boost often offers add-a-line discounts and new-customer deals on this plan for even more savings!

See current Unlimited plan deals

How does the 30-day money-back guarantee work with my wireless plan?

If you’re new to Boost Mobile, you have a full 30 days to test your wireless service risk-free. If you’re not completely satisfied in those first 30 days, just contact Boost Customer Care to initiate a cancellation and request a full refund (including taxes and eligible fees).

To qualify, you must: activate one or more lines on a new account, port in your number from another carrier, enroll in AutoPay, and submit your refund request within 30 days of your account start date.

Add-Ons purchased during those first 30 days (like extra data or international calling features) are also eligible for refund, provided they were bought before you canceled. Services added after you’ve requested cancellation aren’t refundable.

How does device financing with Affirm work with Boost Mobile plans?

When you add a new phone to your cart on the Boost Mobile website, select Affirm as your payment method at checkout. You will be prompted to provide some basic information for a real-time decision. If approved, you can select a payment schedule—typically 3, 6, or 12 months—and complete your purchase immediately.

Learn more about device financing

Which Boost Mobile plans include unlimited data?

All Boost Mobile phone plans include unlimited talk, text and data. Each plan includes a set amount of premium high-speed data, which varies by plan. After you use your monthly premium data, you can continue using unlimited data at reduced speeds or add 10GB of premium data for $10.

What is "premium" data?

Premium data is the high-speed 5G and 4G LTE data included in every Boost Mobile plan. You use it for streaming, video calls, gaming and apps when you're not on Wi-Fi. Boost Mobile's $25 Unlimited plan includes 30GB of premium data each month. Global Connection and Unlimited+ include 40GB, and Unlimited Premium includes 50GB.

All Boost Mobile plans include unlimited data, so you're never cut off and never charged overage fees. If you use all your premium data before the month ends, you can add another 10GB for $10 anytime. Otherwise, you stay connected at reduced speeds until your premium data resets at the start of your next billing cycle.

Can I bring my own phone to any Boost Mobile prepaid plan?

You can bring your own phone to any of Boost Mobile’s Unlimited plans. Before you switch, make sure your device is unlocked and compatible with Boost.

To get started, visit our BYOD page, where you can use your device’s IMEI number to check compatibility with Boost Mobile and then follow a few simple steps to activate service.

Can I keep my current phone number if I switch to Boost Mobile?

Yes, absolutely! When you sign up and activate your Boost Mobile plan, you'll be given the option to port your existing number over from your current carrier. Just make sure your current account remains active until the transfer to Boost Mobile is fully complete.

How do I activate my Boost Mobile service once I sign up?

Activating is easy and can be done entirely online. If you're using an eSIM, you can activate your service immediately after checkout. If you ordered a physical SIM or purchased a new phone from us, simply follow the quick activation instructions included in your package, or visit our online activation page once your device arrives.

For extra help, call our Activation Specialists at (866) 957-8278 or download the Boost Mobile app to manage the process from your phone.

Can I activate my plan using an eSIM, or do I need a physical SIM card?

Boost Mobile supports both! If you are bringing a compatible, unlocked phone that supports eSIM, you can activate your service almost instantly online—no shipping required. If your device requires a traditional physical SIM card, we will ship one to you.

Are there any contracts or credit checks required for an Unlimited plan?

No! Boost Mobile does not require a credit check or an annual contract for our wireless service plans. You can cancel at any time. However, if you choose to finance a new device through Affirm, a soft credit check (which does not impact your credit score) will be required.

Is my phone compatible with my Boost Mobile prepaid plan?

Chances are, yes! Most unlocked 4G LTE and 5G smartphones work perfectly on Boost Mobile. That means you can bring the phone you already own and start saving right away—no need to buy a new device.

To make sure your phone works with Boost, use our quick compatibility checker. In most cases, all it takes is popping in a Boost Mobile SIM card and you’re ready to go. It’s one of the easiest ways to switch to prepaid and keep the phone you love.

Do I need to use AutoPay to qualify for the Unlimited plan?

No, you can still use the Unlimited plan without AutoPay. However, the $25 monthly plan price includes a $5/mo discount for using AutoPay, so your price will be more expensive without it.

What’s the difference between 'prepaid' and 'postpaid' phone plans?

The main difference comes down to when and how you pay:

Prepaid plans: You pay for your service upfront each month. There are no credit checks, no annual contracts, and zero surprise fees—if you hit your data limit, your bill doesn't increase.

Postpaid plans: You are billed at the end of each month for service already used. This typically requires a credit check, carries the risk of unexpected overage charges, and often locks you into multi-year commitments or device financing.
