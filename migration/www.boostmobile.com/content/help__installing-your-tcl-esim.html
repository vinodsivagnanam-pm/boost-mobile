Installing your TCL eSIM

Finish Setting Up Cellular:

If you have an eSIM compatible TCL device, you'll receive a notification on your home screen titled Set up your Boost eSIM once your line is activated. Open this notification and tap Continue. When your eSIM is installed you’ll receive a confirmation message that your activation is complete.

If you can’t find, or accidentally remove the notification from your Home Screen, you can recover your eSIM profile by going to your Settings → Network & Internet. Under eSIM, select + Add SIM → Set up an eSIM → Download. Your mobile plan will be automatically downloaded and activate on the eSIM.
