Refer A Friend

Get paid when your friends and family switch to Boost Mobile! The Boost Mobile Refer A Friend program allows qualifying customers to refer friends and family and get rewarded. Plus, those who join get rewarded too!

What can I get for referring someone to Boost Mobile?

Qualifying Boost Mobile customers can earn up to $100 in bill credits when they refer a friend to
Boost Mobile and the friend completes 30 days of service as an active Boost Mobile customer.

Who’s eligible for the Boost Mobile referral program?

Here are the program eligibility rules:

  • Participants must be single/primary account holders.
  • You must be a Boost Mobile customer for at least 30 days before you can submit
    referrals.
  • Existing customers and referrals on a plan of $40 or more will receive a $20 credit each.
    If the Boost Mobile customer and/or their referral are on a plan of less than $40
    (excluding any plan under $25 per month), both will receive a $10 credit each.
  • Referred friends must remain an active Boost Mobile customer for at least 30 days.
  • Referred friends must provide a valid email address when they activate their Boost
    Mobile account.
  • Referred friends must activate as a new account and not as an added line on an existing
    account.
  • Existing customers cannot refer themselves.
  • Customers must have an active account to earn referral bill credits.

How do I refer someone to Boost Mobile?

  1. Start by registering for Refer A Friend.
  2. Invite your friends to join Boost with your personalized link!
    1. Your friend must use your personalized link to register and sign up with Boost Mobile.
    2. Afterwards, they must return to the referral program page to provide their Boost Mobile phone number.
  3. We'll verify your friend's referral status, and if approved, you both earn $20 or $10 in
    account credits!

How do I receive my referral reward credits?

Once your friend's account has been verified as active for 30 days, you'll be set to receive $20
or $10 in account credits, depending on your plan and your friend's. Referral credits will be
applied to your Boost Mobile account over two months and you'll see the first credit within two
payment cycles of the referral validation.

How many friends can I refer to Boost Mobile per year?

You can refer up to 5 friends per calendar year.
