How do I earn points?

Sign up for Contractor Rewards, purchase and activate a Boost Mobile plan, then upload your receipt to earn points.

What Boost Mobile plans and devices are eligible?

All plans and phone devices are eligible for the Contractor Rewards programs. View plan details here.

When will I receive my points?

You must remain an active paying customer for the full 30-day and 90-day periods to receive both point distributions.

What happens if I cancel my service?

If you cancel your service before reaching either of the 30-day or 90-day milestones, you will not receive payment of points for that respective period.
