Block Phone Numbers

How do I block a phone number?

iPhone

  • Phone: Open the Phone app → Recents. Tap the Info button next to the number you want to block → Block Caller
  • Messages: Open the conversation with the number you want to block. Tap the Phone Number or Contact → Info button → Block Caller.
  • Settings: Go to Settings → Apps → Phone → Blocked Contacts. Tap Add New and enter the Phone Number or Contact you want to block.

Android

  • Phone: Open the Phone app → tap More options ⋮ → Settings → Block numbers. Add a phone number from Recents or Contacts → Done.
  • Messages: Open the Messages app → tap the conversation with the number you want to block. Tap Options ⋮ → Details → Block & report spam.

How do I manage phone numbers that I blocked?

iPhone

  • Open Settings → Apps → Phone → Blocked Contacts to view and manage your blocked numbers. You can unblock a number by swiping left → Unblock.

Android

  • Open Phone → tap More options ⋮ → Settings → Blocked numbers to view and manage your blocked numbers. You can unblock a number by tapping the "X" next to it.
