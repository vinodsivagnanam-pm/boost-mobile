Domestic Roaming

What is roaming?

Roaming service keeps you connected wherever you wander, tapping into partner networks when you're out of Boost Mobile's coverage. Your Boost Mobile plan includes 100 minutes per month of domestic voice roaming, all at no additional charge.

Do I need to do anything to be able to roam on Boost Mobile?

No. As long as your device is capable of roaming, it happens automatically.

Can I call, text, and surf the web when I’m roaming?

Your plan gives you up to 100 domestic voice minutes each month for roaming, plus domestic (SMS) texting while you're on the move. Please note, international voice, SMS texting, and data roaming aren't covered

What happens to my call if I’m roaming and I reach the 100th minute?

Don't sweat it. You can complete a roaming call that's in progress if it exceeds the 100th minute.

Can I get more domestic roaming minutes?

No. When your plan refreshes, we'll give you a new allocation of 100 domestic roaming minutes.

Can I disable domestic roaming?

Yes. You can disable roaming in the device settings.
