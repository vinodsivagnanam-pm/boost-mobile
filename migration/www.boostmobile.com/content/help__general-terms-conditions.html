You agree that these additional requirements (“Mass Arbitration Procedures”) shall apply to your Claim if it is filed as part of a “mass arbitration,” which means twenty-five (25) or more arbitration claims involving the same or similar subject matter and/or issues of law or fact, and where representation of all claimants is the same or coordinated across the cases.
