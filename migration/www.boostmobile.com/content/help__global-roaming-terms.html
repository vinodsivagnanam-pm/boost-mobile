Global Roaming Terms

Global Roaming requires a compatible phone and an eligible monthly domestic plan. Global Roaming is available only for domestic (U.S.) customers traveling internationally in select destinations and not for customers who move, permanently reside or intend extended stays abroad. When traveling, some services such as domestic data applications, voicemail or call-waiting, may not be available or work as expected; available features and speeds may vary by destination. Coverage not available in all areas. The availability of, and access to, emergency calling services (e.g., 911 in the U.S.), may vary by destination. You are advised to familiarize yourself with how to access these services before using your phone for Global Roaming. Networks differ from destination to destination. All destinations may not be available for roaming, and available destinations may change from time to time and from plan to plan. For a list of available destinations log on to the Boost Mobile app (if you are currently a Boost Mobile customer) or call Boost Mobile Customer Care at 833-50-BOOST (833-502-6678). Offer and features are subject to change with or without notice. Other restrictions may apply. You may not use Global Roaming for any purpose, except as explicitly authorized herein, including using excessive data, call times or connectivity as determined by Boost Mobile in its sole and absolute discretion. Any violation of these Global Roaming terms, the Boost Mobile Terms & Conditions or Boost Mobile Policies (including, without limitation, the Acceptable Use Policy) may result in blocking or removal of Global Roaming from Your plan or suspension or termination of Your Service at any time and without notice as determined by Boost Mobile in its sole discretion. For more information on Global Roaming, visit https://help.boostmobile.com/docs/global-roaming.
