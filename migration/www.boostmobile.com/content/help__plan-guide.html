What’s the difference between an unlimited plan and a capped plan?

With an unlimited plan, you never have to worry about running out of data. Use up all your high-speed data for the month? No problem. You may experience slower speeds but still have access to data.

With a plan that has a set amount of data each month, you won’t be able to use data after reaching your limit.

How do I purchase more high-speed data?

Too many Youtube videos this month? No worries, we got you. Add more high-speed data in your account online, in the app or at a Boost Mobile store.
