FAQs

How do I schedule my service?

During checkout, simply select your preferred appointment date and time. Appointments are available 7 days a week, from 8am to 6pm in your local time zone, excluding US national holidays. Appointments need to be scheduled within 30 days of purchase.

How will I know when to expect my Expert?

You’ll receive two notifications from Boost Mobile: one about your appointment and one about its status.

Who are our Boost Mobile Experts and how are they verified?

Our full-time Boost Mobile Experts are here to ensure your safety and peace of mind when selecting Store-to-Door Setup & Activation. Our Experts undergo extensive training and must pass a thorough background check—including driving record and drug screening.

I’m buying new devices, but not all of them qualify for the Store-to-Door service. How do I activate my devices?

As long as you are purchasing at least one approved Store-to-Door device online, our Experts are happy to set up additional devices (including watches and tablets) purchased on the same order during your in-home appointment.

What if I would prefer to have my device shipped to me?

If the Store-to-Door service isn't the best option for you, you can choose to pay in full and receive standard shipping options.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes! For those trying our Boost Mobile experience for the first time, if you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, no hard feelings - just contact Boost Mobile Customer Care at (833) 502-6678 to request a refund.

What if I need to make changes to my appointment?

Just call Boost Mobile Customer Care at (833) 502-6678 if something comes up!
