Language Preferences

How do I update my language preference?

When you log in to your account for the first time, you will set your language preference for receiving notifications in English or Español.

To update your language preference:

Boost Mobile app

Tap Settings at the top right. Under Preferences, tap Notifications → Account.

On the web

Go to Settings → Notifications → Account.
