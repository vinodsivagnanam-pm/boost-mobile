All Your BYOD Questions Answered

What does “BYOD” mean?

“BYOD” means bring your own device, a term most often used to describe bringing your own phone or device to a new phone carrier when switching. At Boost Mobile, we often use the term “BYOP” which means “bring your own phone” and can be used interchangeably with “BYOD”.

Can I bring my own phone to Boost Mobile?

Yes, you can bring your own phone to Boost Mobile and get unlimited talk, text, & data.

To see if your current phone is compatible with Boost Mobile, use our IMEI checker.

What are the benefits of bringing my own phone to Boost Mobile?

Bringing your own phone to Boost Mobile comes with several advantages:

  • Save money – No need to buy a new device; you can use the one you already own and save on any upfront costs or monthly device payments when you switch to Boost.
  • Nationwide coverage – Stay connected wherever you are with Boost Mobile. Check out our Boost Mobile coverage map to see service availability in your neighborhood.
  • Keep your number – In most cases, you can transfer your existing number to your new Boost Mobile plan when you bring your own phone.
Can I bring my own phone to any plan at Boost Mobile?

When you switch to Boost Mobile, you can bring your own phone to any of our Unlimited plans, provided your phone is compatible.

Each of these plans features unlimited talk, text, and data with no contract required. Boost also offers BYOD plans for watches and tablets.

Can I keep my phone number when I bring my own phone to Boost Mobile?

Yes! Boost Mobile allows you to transfer your number from another carrier when you bring your own phone to one of our plans.

Can I bring my own tablet or watch to Boost Mobile?

Yes! You can bring your own tablet and watch when you switch to a Boost Mobile plan.
