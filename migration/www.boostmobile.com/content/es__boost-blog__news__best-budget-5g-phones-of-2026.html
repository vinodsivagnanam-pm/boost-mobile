Which 5G Phone Is Best for Travelers?

Whether you're taking a weekend trip across the border or flying overseas, global connectivity is a must. Roaming performance comes down to two things: cellular band support (the specific radio frequencies a phone can connect to worldwide) and eSIM flexibility (the ability to download local data plans digitally).

The Winners: Apple iPhone 17e & iPhone 16e

When it comes to international travel, Apple's budget lineup is unmatched. Both the iPhone 17e and iPhone 16e are designed as universal "world phones." They pack over 25 distinct 5G and LTE bands into a single device, meaning they connect seamlessly to local carrier networks across more than 200 countries. Because they utilize advanced dual-active eSIM technology (allowing you to store eight or more digital SIM profiles), you can instantly download a local prepaid data plan when you land abroad while keeping your primary Boost Mobile number active for important calls and two-factor authentication texts.

The Samsung Galaxy A27 5G is a strong runner-up, offering reliable roaming across North America and Europe with solid eSIM support. However, midrange Android devices occasionally feature regional band locks that can limit 5G speeds in parts of Asia or South America compared to the universal modem architecture found in the iPhones. The moto g (2026) is best suited for domestic travel and North American roaming (Canada and Mexico), as its modem prioritizes U.S. cellular frequencies.
