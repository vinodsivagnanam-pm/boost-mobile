Basic Troubleshooting

Having trouble calling, texting, or using data? No worries. Here are some quick tips to help you fix common issues.

Follow these steps first

  1. Check your account online or in the Boost Mobile app to make sure your account is in good standing and not suspended for non-payment.
  2. If you have a physical SIM card, remove and reinsert your SIM card.
  3. In your phone's settings:
    1. Make sure your software is up to date.
    2. Make sure Airplane Mode is off.
    3. Make sure Do Not Disturb is off.
  4. Turn your phone off and back on.

For Android Users - Make sure you’ve downloaded the Boost Config app to ensure your network settings are up to date.
