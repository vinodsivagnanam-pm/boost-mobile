Track Your Usage

See how much high-speed data you've used, explore add-on options for your line or turn on device settings to stretch your monthly data further.

Track your data usage

  • Boost Mobile app

    On the line you want to check, tap View Details → My Usage.

  • On the web

    Log in to your Dashboard to see your usage under My Lines.

Need to add more data?

  • Boost Mobile app

    Find the line you want to add more Premium Data. Tap View Details → My Usage → Get More Data → select a High-Speed Data pack.

  • On the web

    On your Dashboard, select Add More Data → Select a Line → choose your High-Speed Data pack.

How can I lower my data usage?

Reduce background app activity, stop automatic downloads and more right from your device settings:

  • iPhone - Learn how to Enable Low Data Mode on iPhone
  • Android - Select your device in our step-by step guides and search for "Data Saver"
