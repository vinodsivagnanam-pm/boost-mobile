FAQs

Do I need a data plan for my smartwatch?

While a data plan for your smartwatch isn’t required, having one unlocks its full potential, allowing you to talk, text, and get online without connecting to your phone.

Without a data plan, your watch can only connect through your smartphone’s Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, which can limit your options when your phone isn’t nearby. With a Boost Mobile smartwatch data plan, your watch can make and receive calls, send texts, stream music, use GPS, and access apps all on its own, so you never have to worry about leaving your phone behind. For most users who want true on-the-go freedom, adding a smartwatch data plan is the best choice for staying connected wherever the world takes you.

Can I bring my own device when signing up for a smartwatch plan?

Yes! With Boost Mobile’s BYOP program, you can bring your own Apple Watch and connect it to one of our affordable smartwatch data plans. As long as your device features cellular capabilities, you can enjoy unlimited talk, text, and data on your smartwatch for just $10/mo.

Bringing your own device makes it easy to keep the smartwatch you already love while getting the freedom of an affordable Boost Mobile data plan.

Is my smartwatch compatible with a Boost Mobile data plan?

Most popular smartwatches, including the newest Apple Watch models, are compatible with Boost Mobile data plans. To ensure your smartwatch works on our network, it must be an unlocked device that supports cellular connectivity.

If your watch meets these requirements, you can add it to a Boost Mobile smartwatch data plan for just $10/month and enjoy unlimited talk, text, and data. Checking compatibility is simple, just verify your device with its IMEI number and we can get you started with a smartwatch data plan.
