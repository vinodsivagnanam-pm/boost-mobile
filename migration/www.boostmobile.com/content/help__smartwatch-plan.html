Smartwatch Plan

Specific smartwatches (the “Watch”) may not be available. Offer and Offer Terms subject to modification or termination at any time in Boost Mobile’s sole discretion. Taxes on full pre-credit retail price of the Watch (the “List Price”) due at sale. Monthly $10 fees for watch includes unlimited talk, text, data and a credit for the Watch model (credit value varies depending on the specific promotional offer at time of purchase) and does not include taxes, regulatory fees or surcharges. Available only for qualified customers who finance the Watch that is linked to any Boost Mobile wireless plan. Deleting your linked wireless plan may cause an increase in the fees. If customer fails to activate the Watch within 30 days following the date the Watch is purchased, Boost Mobile may, in its sole discretion, charge customer full List Price of the purchased Watch using the payment method on file. Customers using more than 2GB of data allotment may experience slower speeds. Unlimited data in US only. Coverage not available in some areas; Boost Mobile is not responsible for its’ partners’ networks. 36-Month Finance Agreement: plan requires a 36-month finance agreement with Boost Mobile for the Watch included. The customer’s credit value will be applied in equal amounts over a 36-month period depending as long as customer remains active in good standing on any Boost Mobile wireless plan. If customer’s Watch plan is canceled, terminated or suspended (including cancellation by customer), credits are forfeited and customer will be immediately responsible for the outstanding balance due on the finance agreement. See finance agreement for full details. Early payment forfeits remaining credits.
