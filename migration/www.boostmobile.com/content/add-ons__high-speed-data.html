FAQs

How do I add High-Speed Data to my current plan?

You can add extra high-speed data online through your Boost Mobile account (Go to My Add-Ons, explore Add-Ons, High Speed Data 10GB), in the Boost Mobile app (Go to Manage Add-Ons, under Services and Data Packs), or by contacting Boost Customer Care at (833) 502-6678.

Can I use my High-Speed Add-On with my Hotspot Add-On?

Yup! If you have the Data Hotspot Add-On, any additional high-speed data you purchase can be used with your hotspot.

How can I cancel this Add-On?

If you have a Recurring Data Pack, you can cancel it anytime through your Boost Mobile account, the Boost Mobile App or by contacting Customer Care at (833) 502-6678.
