The Global Connection Plan includes unlimited talk, text and data in the United States, Global Talk and Text, 40 GB high-speed data, and 1 GB of Global Roaming per month when travelling in select locations, all for just $40/mo. (with autopay); without autopay, the cost is $45 per month.

Bring your own phone or purchase a phone from us. Specific phone models may not be available. Prices vary based on phone model selected. Offer and Offer Terms subject to modification or termination at any time in Boost Mobile’s sole discretion. Taxes on full pre-credit retail price of phone (the “List Price”) and first month’s payment due at sale. Monthly fee includes unlimited talk, text and data, and does not include taxes, regulatory fees or surcharges. Available only for qualified customers. Phone is extra. Switching plans may cause you to lose current plan, promotional offers, or feature benefits. Max 10 lines. If a customer fails to activate the wireless service plan within 30 days following the date a phone is purchased, Boost Mobile will charge the customer the full List Price of the purchased phone using the payment method on file. Customers using more than 40GB of data allotment may experience reduced speeds for the remainder of the billing cycle. Unlimited data in U.S. only. Coverage not available in some areas; Boost Mobile is not responsible for its partners’ networks.

Global Talk & Text: Allows enrolled customers to talk and text to more than 190 countries in accordance with the International Long Distance Consumer Agreement. Maximum usage varies by country.

Global Roaming: Subject to the Global Roaming Terms, customers will receive 1 GB of high-speed international data per month. Available only for domestic (U.S.) customers temporarily traveling internationally in select destinations. When traveling, some services such as domestic data applications, voicemail or call-waiting, may not be available or work as expected; available features and speeds may vary by destination. Coverage not available in all areas.

Limited-time plan, subject to change or termination at any time in Boost Mobile’s sole discretion. Plan terms are in addition to, and in no way limitation of, Boost Mobile Terms and Conditions.
