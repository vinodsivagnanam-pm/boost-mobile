Boost Mobile Tablet Plans

Specific tablet devices (the “Tablet”) may not be available. Offer and Offer Terms subject to modification or termination at any time in Boost Mobile’s sole discretion. Taxes on full pre-credit retail price of the Tablet (the “List Price”) due at sale. Monthly fees for the Tablet include unlimited data and, if applicable under the specific offer or promotion, a credit for the Tablet model (credit values vary) and does not include taxes, regulatory fees or surcharges. Available only for qualified customers who finance a Tablet linked to a Boost Mobile wireless plan. Deleting your linked wireless plan may cause an increase in the fees. If customer fails to activate the Tablet on a Boost Mobile wireless plan within 30 days following the date it is purchased, Boost Mobile will charge customer full List Price of the purchased Tablet using the payment method on file. Customers using more than 30GB of data allotment may experience slower speeds. Unlimited data in US only. Coverage not available in some areas; Boost Mobile is not responsible for its’ partners’ networks. 36-Month Finance Agreement: plan requires a 36-month finance agreement with Boost Mobile for the Tablet included. The customer’s credit value, if any, will be applied in equal amounts over a 36-month period as long as customer remains active in good standing on any Boost Mobile wireless plan. If customer’s plan is canceled, terminated or suspended (including cancellation by customer), any credits are forfeited and customer will be immediately responsible for the outstanding balance due on the finance agreement. See finance agreement for full details. Early payment forfeits remaining credits (if applicable).
