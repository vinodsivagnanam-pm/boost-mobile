FAQs

Are Boost Mobile hotspot plans unlimited?

Your Boost Mobile device hotspot will pull directly from your existing high-speed monthly allotment. Once you run out of high-speed data, you can add more for an additional cost. To learn more about adding additional data to your Boost Mobile plan, visit our High-Speed Data Add-Ons page.

How do I connect devices to my Boost Mobile Wi-Fi hotspot?

In your hotspot ready device, go to Settings > Connections > Mobile Hotspots and ensure the mobile hotspot option is enabled. On your other devices, select your hotspot devices name in the Wi-Fi settings to connect.

What is a mobile hotspot?

A mobile hotspot is a phone or device that acts as a portable Wi-Fi hub, allowing your other devices (laptops, tablets or watches) to connect to the internet no matter where you are.

What happens if I run out of data on my Boost Mobile hotspot?

When you run out of high-speed data on your Boost Mobile hotspot, you can add an additional 10GB of premium data for just $10.
These one-time data packs expire 30 days after purchase, or when you use the additional allotment. The additional data will be available for both your regular device data and hotspot availability.
