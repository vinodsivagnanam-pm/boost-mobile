North America Connect

$10/mo

Stay connected with family & friends in Canada or Mexico. Add North America Connect in your account online, in the Boost Mobile app or by contacting Boost Customer Care.

Here’s what you’ll get:

  • Unlimited talk & text from the US to Mexico/Canada
  • Unlimited talk & text across North America while roaming
  • 5GB of data while roaming in Mexico/Canada

North America Connect is included at no additional cost with the Unlimited Premium and Infinite Access plans.

Note: "data roaming" must be turned on in your device's settings for this feature to function properly.

What does roaming mean?

When we talk about roaming in Canada/Mexico it means you're able to use services while traveling in those countries.

How do I call the US from Mexico or Canada?

To call someone in the US while you're roaming in Mexico or Canada, dial +1 (XXX) XXX-XXXX.

Who provides cellular coverage for Boost Mobile in Mexico?

Telefonica provides Boost Mobile roaming coverage in Mexico.

Can I call Emergency Services in Mexico?

Yes, you can call local Mexico Emergency Services (066) while traveling in Mexico. You'll receive emergency service in Spanish at your Mexico location. Emergency service calls will not incur any access fee or roaming charges.

What happens if I run out of roaming data while I'm in Mexico or Canada?

No problem! You can easily add more data in the Boost Mobile app or your account online for only $10 per additional GB (up to an additional 9GB). Any data you don’t use on your trip can still be used for a full 30 days from when it was added.

Important facts about North America Connect

  • Primary usage must occur in the United States. Calls, texts or data roaming while in Mexico cannot exceed 50% of total usage over time or your service may be terminated.
  • Mexico/Canada roaming coverage and service is not available everywhere.
  • Mexico/Canada roaming is only available on compatible LTE/GSM devices.
  • Data speeds are dependent on the local carriers network.
