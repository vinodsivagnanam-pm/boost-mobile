What are Find My and Activation Lock?

Find My is an Apple service that helps you locate your devices. When you turn on Find My, it automatically enables a security feature called Activation Lock.

Activation Lock is designed to prevent anyone else from using your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch if it is ever lost or stolen. It secures your device by requiring your Apple Account password before anyone can sign out of iCloud, erase the device, or reactivate it.

Activation Lock MUST BE DISABLED before you trade in your device so that the device can be activated by a new user.
